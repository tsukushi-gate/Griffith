**A few dozen logic gates clear Mario 1-1, make a bug walk, and make a snake swim.**

Nothing on this page learned neural-network weights.
Each controller is **a circuit of two-input logic gates (AND, OR, XOR, ...)**, grown generation by generation with neuroevolution (NEAT).
The circuits stay small, yet they find their own way to beat a game or move a body.

## What is going on

- Screen and sensor values are converted into **bit vectors** and fed into a logic-gate circuit
- The circuit's output bits pick an action such as "run right", "jump", or "bend this joint"
- NEAT changes **the shape of the circuit itself** (which gates exist and how they are wired) every generation and keeps the individuals that do well

What remains after evolution is not a weight table but **a logic circuit small enough to read**.
For several environments you can follow it all the way down to a circuit diagram and Boolean formulas. The mechanics are in [How It Works](/en/showcase/how-it-works/).

## An honest scorecard

Logic circuits do not always win. With the same observations and the same evaluation, we compared them against classic NEAT with real-valued weights (NN). The winner depends on the environment.

| Environment | Logic circuit (DLGN) | NN | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flappy Bird | Solved in 2 of 2 runs with 11 gates | Not solved even after 900 generations | **Logic circuit wins** |
| 2D race car | Solved in 2 of 2 runs with 22-23 gates | Not solved in either run | **Logic circuit wins** |
| Super Mario 1-1 | 1 of 2 runs cleared (32 gates) | 1 of 2 runs cleared (51 parameters) | Tie |
| Bug walker | Walks with 32 gates (distance 20.6) | Walks with 16 parameters (21.1) | About even |
| CartPole | Solved immediately with 8 gates | Solved immediately with 4 parameters | Tie |
| LunarLander | Failed independent verification | Lands with 12-33 parameters | **NN wins** |

Gate counts and parameter counts are different units, so they cannot be divided into a ratio. Detailed comparisons, including where the circuits lost, are in [Creatures & Robots](/en/showcase/creatures/) and [Games & Control](/en/showcase/games/).
