Solving MNIST is only the starting point. This page lines up measurements of **how far an
architecture can go on logic gates alone**, separating what works from what does not. Nothing has
been cherry-picked to make the numbers look good: **models that are not yet calibrated, and models
that collapsed, appear in the same tables**.

A one-line index that also covers the models from the experimental branches is in the
[Model Zoo](/en/reference/model-zoo/).

:::caution[How to read these numbers]
Unless stated otherwise, the values are **single-seed, single-configuration measurements**; seed
variance was not taken. Metrics are reported primarily on the **hard** side (the pure Boolean
circuit after discretization). Soft values (the continuous relaxation used during training) appear
only so the difference is visible.
:::

## 1. Image classification

| Model | Dataset | hard | Conditions / environment |
|---|---|---|---|
| DLGN (softmax N=2, 6 layers × 8,000) | MNIST 20×20 | **0.9784** | A10G, 200k iter, 2026-07-02 |
| DLGN (Hard-ST selection, 6 layers × 64,000) | MNIST 28×28 | 0.9802 | RunPod GPU, 50k iter, 2026-04-15 |
| DLGN (softmax N=2, 6 layers × 16,000) | MNIST 28×28 | 0.9774 | RTX 5090, 50ep, 2026-04-20 |
| BinaryBrain style (raw+BN+Binarize, N=6) | MNIST | 0.9483 | 14,516 gates, 10ep, 2026-04-20 |
| Conv-DLGN (after the `softmax16g` fix) | CIFAR-10 | 0.5808 | 0.4921 before the fix (+8.9pt), 2026-07-05 |
| LogicMixer (a logic MLP-Mixer) | MNIST | saturates at 0.671 | 20k steps, **needs calibration** |
| LogicResNet | MNIST | 0.113 (near chance) | 3ep, loss does decrease. **Unresolved; treated as a τ/scaling calibration problem** |

CIFAR-10 falls well below the MNIST-class numbers. **As task difficulty rises, both the gate budget
and the architecture start to matter** — that is where things stand, and it is recorded here without
embellishment.

Conv-DLGN's +8.9pt came from fixing a structural flaw in `LutConv2d` (a 4-entry simplex cannot
express pass-through / OR / XOR, and the residual initialization was also decaying) by moving to
`softmax16g`. It is a concrete case of **missing expressiveness in a parameterization killing
accuracy at the architecture level**, and it matches
[The experiment behind the parameterization rule](/en/explanation/results/parameterization-study/).

## 2. Language models (autoregressive)

A "ladder" measuring, step by step, how much of a Transformer can be turned into a circuit, on a
character-level language model over Tiny Shakespeare (vocab 65, T=64, 20k steps, A10G, seed=0).

| Rung | What was turned into a circuit | hard ppl |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | nothing (all FP; establishes the ceiling) | 4.94 |
| L0b | FFN only | ≈7.9 |
| L1 | + q/k/v projections as circuits | 19.24 |
| L2 | + scores as XNOR-popcount | 19.39 |
| L3-C | + normalization as an argmax MUX selector (attention fully circuit-ized) | 15.54 |
| **L3-C + Gumbel** | same + Gumbel gate-selection noise | **13.31** |

Two things stand out.

- **The cost of circuit-ization is not monotone.** L1/L2 fall to the 19s, then L3-C — which replaces
  normalization with a MUX — recovers to 15.5. Softmax flattening is not required, and **attention
  works as a "learned multiplexer"**. A controlled experiment on sequential MNIST agrees: MUX
  selection (entropy=0) matched softmax (entropy=2.7) in accuracy (0.8605 vs. 0.8606).
- **The gap to the FP ceiling (4.94) is still wide.** hard ppl 13.31 shows a language model can run
  as a logic circuit, but it is not level with the FP implementation.

For the discretization-gap side of this, see
[The discretization gap in practice](/en/explanation/results/discretization-gap/).

## 3. Sequence models (recurrent, seq2seq)

| Model | Task | Result | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| LogicLSTM | delayed copy (T=8) | **hard acc 100% (all seeds, ≤200 steps)** | An Elman-type model under the same conditions is ≈0.86 even at 800 steps |
| LogicReservoir (frozen random circuit) | delayed bit recall | **hard acc 100% (all seeds) from training the readout alone** | the circuit body is frozen and purely Boolean |
| DLGNAttentionTranslator | copy (seq_len=16) | soft 71.66% / hard 25.77% | 50,000 steps, 156,032 parameters / 9,352 gates |
| RDDLGN (reimplementation of prior work) | copy (seq_len=16) | soft 22.54% / hard 15.54% | same conditions, 180,608 parameters / 10,888 gates |

**The Translator with cross-attention achieved 3.18× RDDLGN's soft accuracy and 1.66× its hard
accuracy with 13.6% fewer parameters** (2026-04-21). That is evidence the information bottleneck of
RDDLGN's attention-free structure was really biting. Even so, hard 25.77% is not a practical level:
**seq2seq is currently the area most vulnerable to discretization**.

On gating, tests guarantee that `LogicLSTM`'s gates discretize to `{0,1}` at eval time, collapsing
into a strictly Boolean MUX circuit. **Gating — as MUX / AND masking — is inherently compatible with
Boolean operations**, which is what the measurements show.

## 4. Detection and dense prediction

| Model | Task | hard | Environment |
|---|---|---|---|
| CenterNetLUT | Multi-MNIST detection (96×96, 1 object) | **mAP 0.5425** (epoch 78, **pre-fix figure, awaiting re-measurement**) | RTX 5090, 1,000ep, 11h45m, 13,944 parameters |
| LogicUNet | 8×8 toy segmentation | IoU 0.875 | **needs calibration** |
| LogicUNet | full MNIST masks | IoU 0.373 | **needs calibration** |

CenterNetLUT peaks at epoch 78 and then becomes unstable. It is an unusual case where hard far
exceeds soft (mAP 0.1791), and **you will miss the peak unless model selection is based on the hard
side**.

## 5. Generative models

| Model | Task | Result | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| LogicBinaryDiffusion (forward process, reverse process and denoiser all Boolean circuits) | binary MNIST | acc@t1 0.886, denoising margin +0.377 (passthrough baseline 0.499) | 4ep, **3,937** parameters total |
| LogicVAE (binary latent, ST Bernoulli) | binary MNIST | reconstruction 193.6 nat/image, KL 0.448, active_bits 0.99 (no posterior collapse) | 30ep |
| LutAutoencoder (`LutLayer` only) | MNIST reconstruction | Test MSE (hard) **0.0863** (best, epoch 63) / soft 0.0438 at epoch 65 | Apple M4 Pro (MPS), k=8,000, bottleneck=256, 65ep (stopped early), 30,784 gates |
| LogicGAN | 8×8 binary patterns | diverges without tuning; stabilization measures bring it to a standoff | **research problem** (only structural-contract and dynamics smoke tests) |

The diffusion model's **3,937 parameters** is a number worth noting. Because "sampling = comparator"
and "adding noise = XOR", the generative process closes entirely within logic circuits.

## 6. Text classification (real data)

A LUT-based TextCNN on WRIME (sentiment analysis of Japanese social-media posts). All values are
hard-side.

| Task | Result | Comparison |
|---|---|---|
| binary classification | Accuracy **82%** | — |
| 8-emotion binary classification | Accuracy **83.4%** / mean F1 **0.367** | DeBERTa-v3-base 87.0% / F1 0.630 |

The 8-emotion version uses about 2.08M parameters (1/88 of DeBERTa) for −3.6pt Accuracy and −0.263
F1. **Accuracy comes close, but F1 lags badly.** Minority classes are being dropped, and "close
accuracy" is explicitly not "equivalent". Training took about 15 minutes on GPU.

## 7. Conversion from pretrained models

Can existing floating-point models be converted into logic gates (Track D)?

| Route | Result |
|---|---|
| pruned transcription + STE fine-tune + int4 QAT + adder-tree lowering (MNIST) | **within −2% of the teacher (hard 0.9558, strict lowering)**. The circuit has 418k LUT outputs |
| the same recipe transferred to Fashion-MNIST | 0.8196 (passes with fan-in 48 + bits 6) |
| KD from a strong teacher (public ResNet18 on CIFAR-10, 0.9498) | **+2.3pt** over scratch for a dense DLGN |
| native DLGN conversion from a weak teacher (MNIST) | all 3 routes **below scratch** (a negative result) |
| KD from public DistilBERT to SST-2 sentiment classification | hard accuracy **+7.3pt** (73.4% → 80.7%, 88.4% recovery) |
| data-free distillation from public nanoGPT to a character LM | RDDLGN actual BPC **2.71** (0.51 bit behind the teacher) |

The central finding here is the scaling hypothesis: **the gap in capability between teacher and
student is the source of distillation's gains.** Conversion from a weak teacher loses to training
from scratch. Conversely, the fact that language-generation conversion succeeded even with no
training data (only teacher-generated text) points to a path when the original data is unavailable
in production.

## 9. Where things stand

- **Solidly working**: image classification (MNIST class), recurrent tasks such as delayed copy,
  simple detection settings, generative models with binary latents, text classification.
- **Working, but short of FP**: autoregressive language models (hard ppl 13.31 against an FP ceiling
  of 4.94), CIFAR-10-class image classification (0.5808).
- **Unachieved / under calibration**: LogicResNet (≈chance), LogicMixer (saturates at 0.671),
  LogicUNet (IoU 0.373 on full MNIST), LogicGAN (stabilization is a research problem).
- **The common bottleneck is the discretization gap**, and the current state of the remedies is
  collected in [The discretization gap in practice](/en/explanation/results/discretization-gap/).
